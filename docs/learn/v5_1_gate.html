TRACE v5.1 gate: fixing the mean model was enough

PASS   2026-10-31 ET · prereg b33f507 · implementation merge 665939b

Short version. We swapped one piece of v5, the model for the average outcome, from a binned booster to a plain straight-line (least-squares) fit. Nothing else changed and nothing was tuned. On fresh seeds, v5.1 beat the best rival in all four cells, and it put the change in the right bucket every time. The gate passes.

What was tested

When an outcome's distribution changes between two periods, TRACE splits the change into two parts: composition (the inputs moved) and response (the way the outcome reacts to the inputs changed). In the two test scenarios, all of the planted change is composition: the skew of the outcome changes (skew_comp) or the tail weight changes (tail_comp), only because the inputs shifted.

v5 failed its first gate. A diagnosis suggested the culprit was the mean model, not the mixture idea itself. v5.1 tests exactly that, and only that: fit the mean with ordinary least squares (an intercept plus every input) in each period, keep everything else the same.

The rules, fixed in advance

  • Four cells: both scenarios at n = 2,000 (50 seeds, 9700–9749, 5 inputs) and at n = 20,000 (20 seeds, 9800–9819, 20 inputs). None of these seeds had been used before.
  • For each seed, compare v5.1's error with the better of the two rivals (DFL reweighting and CFM) on the same data. Call the difference d; negative means v5.1 did better.
  • BEST if the 95% upper bound on the average d is below zero. Non-inferior if it is below a small margin δ (10% of the planted effect). Two ways of computing the bound (a t-interval and a bootstrap) both had to agree; the larger was used.
  • Role check: the composition piece must be larger than the response piece, and swapping the two must not reduce the error.
  • Any failed cell, any failed role check, or any cell unfinished within 30 minutes would shelve the v5 line.

How to read this

Errors shrink from n = 2,000 to n = 20,000, as an estimator that is on target should. CFM was the stronger rival in every cell, and v5.1 roughly halved its error. In v5, tail_comp had the roles backwards. With the straight-line mean, the composition piece is now about 2.5 to 5 times the response piece.

What this does not show. The test data has a straight-line mean, so a straight-line fit is the right shape by construction. The pass shows the v5 mixture works once the mean model is right. It says nothing yet about curved (non-linear) means, structure-only cells, coverage, real-data benchmarks, or speed. Those need their own pre-registered tests.

How to use it

traceot.explain_v5_1(x_a, y_a, x_b, y_b), or traceot.explain_v5(..., mean_model="ols"). The default explain_v5 is unchanged.
