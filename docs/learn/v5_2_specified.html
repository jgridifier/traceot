What was asked

V5.1 passed its four-cell gate with a straight-line mean, on a simulator whose mean is itself a straight line. That result does not show what happens when the mean is curved. This follow-up asks one narrower question: if the mean is curved and the location model is the correct family (least squares on the straight-line terms plus the exact two curved terms), does the same mixture still pass the same gate?

A pass requires every cell to be BEST or NON-INFERIOR against DFL and CFM after Holm, and to pass the role check. The kill rule parks the claim if a cell fails accuracy, fails the role check, or runs out of time. A missing-rival outcome is not in that list; it is treated the same way as a calibration stop: H is left undecided, and no park file is written.

Why the rivals failed, and why they were not retried

The pass ran in a fresh Python 3.13 environment that had the TRACE library, numpy and scipy, but not scikit-learn. Both rivals import it. That should have been checked before the pass started. It was not. A different environment on the same machine already had scikit-learn 1.9.1. This is an environment miss, not a method failure.

The pre-registration says no task is dropped, retried, or re-run, and there are no re-runs to chase a bar. The 140 TRACE fits and the 280 rival error records are the official pass. Seeds 11000–11049 and 11100–11119 are spent. A complete accuracy test against DFL and CFM would need a new pre-registration on fresh seeds. This page does not open one.
