TRACE v5.1 on structure-only and mixed cells: the gate ran once, and H is rejected

FAIL · claim parked   2026-11-17, 8:09 AM ET · prereg 85a8fcc · implementation 665939b · runner 5562b22

Short version. After a successful pre-flight — including a bit-identity check of explain_v5_1 against the V5.1 merge on one seed-99 skew_struct draw — and a successful rehearsal of the full pipeline on throwaway seeds 97 and 98, the frozen test ran once, on fresh seeds 14000–14049 and 14100–14119. All 210 tasks finished in 19.3 seconds, well under the 30-minute cap. The two mixed cells passed: TRACE was BEST against DFL and CFM after Holm, and it got the signs right. All six role checks passed. But three of the four structure-only cells failed the false-attribution check (the composition piece was not small enough when its planted truth is about 0), and tail_struct at n = 2 000 also failed the accuracy bar. Under the pre-registered kill rule, that is a FAIL: the hypothesis is rejected, and the widened claim is parked. The validated V5.1 claim remains the one on composition-only cells. There are no re-runs.

Why this test existed

The original V5.1 gate only used composition cells: the covariates change and the residual law stays put. A method that labelled every shift “composition” would also have passed that gate. This suite was the direct check of that hole. On structure-only cells the covariates have the same law in both periods and only the residual law changes, so the composition truth is about 0. On the mixed cell both roles are planted, with opposite signs.

Nothing in the method was changed for this test. Four later commits had touched the Rust core, so pre-flight first showed that explain_v5_1 output on the seed-99 draw was bit-identical to the V5.1 merge. Sealed seeds 9600–9619 were not used. The coverage study seeds 10400–10799 were not used.

How to read this

The mixed cells are the clean pass: when both roles are planted with opposite signs, V5.1 is clearly better than DFL and CFM, and it assigns a positive composition piece and a negative structure piece, as planted. The role check also passed on every structure-only cell: the structure piece was larger than the composition piece, and swapping the two labels made the error worse, not better.

The scored miss is false attribution. On a structure-only cell the planted composition truth is about 0, so the composition piece is supposed to stay inside a small margin δ. The signed average of that piece is in fact close to zero. The bar, though, is on the average of the absolute value. That average was not small enough on three of the four structure-only cells. Separately, on tail_struct at n = 2 000, TRACE was slightly worse on average than the best rival (DFL), and the upper bound sat above δ, so accuracy failed as well.

Those failures sink the hypothesis. The two cells that passed every check cannot rescue it. Nothing was retried.

What this does not undo. The original V5.1 gate on skew_comp and tail_comp still stands (composition-cell results): BEST against DFL and CFM, role check passing, linear-mean simulator only. The coverage PASS is also untouched. This failure narrows the public claim to those composition-only cells. It says nothing new about curved means (V5.2 and V5.2c remain parked), ShiftBench, speed, or per-feature shares.
