(b) Mandatory pre-flight

Before any gate seed is touched, in the exact environment that will run the study: import TRACE and both rivals; run one smoke fit of each method on a non-gate seed (seed 99, already used for the oracle truth, not a gate index); and record package versions (Python, TRACE, numpy, scipy, scikit-learn). Any failed import, failed smoke fit, or missing package aborts: the gate seeds are not used, the hypothesis is left undecided, and it is not a fail and not a park. Because the seeds were not consumed, they may still be used after the environment is repaired and a later pre-flight succeeds. Once a successful pre-flight is followed by the start of the gate pass, the seeds are spent and are not retried.

(c) A descriptive item, reported, not scored

On the first specified-family pass, TRACE's mean composition piece on tail_comp sat well below the planted value. That is acknowledged here and will be reported again, for every cell, as a secondary descriptive note. It is not a scored bar: it does not enter Holm, BEST, NON-INFERIOR, the role check, or the pass/fail decision, and it is not used to change the hypothesis.

Kill rule. If any cell misses the accuracy bar, fails the role check, or runs out of time, the claim is parked (PARK_V5_2B_SPECIFIED.md), and nothing is re-run, re-seeded, or re-tuned. V5.1’s straight-line claim is unchanged either way, and the v5.2 park stays in place. If the simulator cannot be calibrated, or if the pre-flight aborts, the gate seeds are not run and the hypothesis is left undecided — it is not redesigned.

What this will not claim

  • A PASS does not say a spline, a GAM, or any approximating model works. The location family is correct by construction; that is the point of the test, and its limit.
  • It does not un-park v5.2, and it does not re-run v5.2’s seeds or the first specified-family seeds.
  • It does not unseal 9600–9619, and it does not re-run the coverage study.
  • Structure-only cells, mixed cells, speed, and real data are out of scope.
