Short version. After a successful pre-flight (in a spawned child, with scikit-learn present) and a successful rehearsal of the full pipeline on throwaway seeds 97 and 98, the frozen test ran once, on fresh seeds 13000–13049 and 13100–13119. All 140 tasks finished in 12.7 seconds, well under the 30-minute cap. The two skewed-composition cells passed: TRACE was BEST against DFL and CFM after Holm, and the role check passed. Under the pre-registered kill rule, that is a FAIL: the hypothesis is rejected, and the specified-family claim is parked. The seeds were not retried. Nothing is amended.

What was asked

This is a replication of the V5.2 specified-family test, on fresh seeds, with a spawn-safe harness, a mandatory rehearsal, and a five-minute watchdog. The question is unchanged: if the mean is curved and the location model is the correct family (least squares on the straight-line terms plus the exact two curved terms), does the v5.1 mixture still pass the same four-cell gate against DFL and CFM?

A pass requires every cell to be BEST or NON-INFERIOR after Holm, and to pass the role check. Both the confidence-interval verdict and the matching Holm-adjusted p-value must hold. The kill rule parks the claim if any cell fails accuracy, fails the role check, or runs out of time.

The best rival in every cell was CFM. Mean d is TRACE's error minus the best rival's error: negative means TRACE was more accurate on average. “Upper bound” is the larger of the one-sided 95% t and bootstrap bounds. BEST requires that bound to be below zero, and the Holm p-value for a BEST claim to be below 0.05. NON-INFERIOR requires the bound to be below δ and the Holm p-value for a non-inferiority claim to be below 0.05. Both the bound and the p-value are required.

Why the two heavy-tail cells failed

On tail_comp at n = 2,000, TRACE's mean error (0.01043) was slightly worse than CFM’s (0.01172). The average gap is small (+0.00073), but the upper bound (0.00240) sits well above a tight non-inferiority margin (δ = 0.000356). The interval itself is a fail. The Holm p-values (0.646 for non-inferiority, 0.767 for best) agree.

On tail_comp at n = 20,000, TRACE was better on average (0.003511 against CFM’s 0.004315). The upper bound (0.000291) is under δ (0.000356), which would be NON-INFERIOR on the interval alone. The Holm-adjusted p-value for that non-inferiority claim is 0.0814, which is not below 0.05. The frozen rule requires both, so the cell is a fail. This is not a close-enough, and it is not rounded to a pass.

How the pass was run

  • Pre-flight: ran in a spawned child, before any gate seed was touched. TRACE, DFL, CFM and scikit-learn 1.9.1 all imported and completed a smoke fit on non-gate seed 99. The parent process never called TRACE.
  • Rehearsal: the full parallel pipeline, all three methods, on throwaway seeds 98 and 97 (8 tasks, same n and p as the cells), finished 8/8 in 2.9 seconds. Those records are in a separate file and were not scored.
  • Gate: a spawn pool of 8 workers, 140 tasks, 12.7 seconds, 30-minute cap, five-minute watchdog (it did not trip). No task was dropped, retried, or re-run. Seeds 13000–13049 and 13100–13119 are spent.
