TRACE v5.1: why v5 failed, and the one test we will run next

PRE-REGISTERED   2026-10-30 ET · gate not run

Recap. The v5 first gate FAILED on the n=2000 dev seeds. On skew_comp v5 erred 0.0270 vs CFM 0.0222; on tail_comp it was close on error but put more weight on the wrong role (|ĉ| 0.0031 < |ŝ| 0.0049). Both upper bounds missed the non-inferiority margin. The v5 gate is closed.

What we wanted to know

v5 has two parts: (1) a model of how the outcome depends on the inputs in each period, and (2) a recipe that mixes those models over the other period's inputs and turns the result back into a distribution. Which part broke? We guessed part (1) assumed every row has the same spread and shape. We checked instead of guessing.

How we checked (exploratory — same spent seeds 9500–9549)

The data come from a simulator, so we know the true model. We kept the v5 recipe exactly and fed it different part-(1) models:

  • True model — the simulator's real mean and noise.
  • True model, exact — same, but without the 256-point grid, to measure grid error alone.
  • Linear fit — a plain least-squares line estimated from the data, nothing known in advance.

What that shows

  • The mixing recipe is fine. Given the right model, it beats the rivals in both cells. Grid error is about one millionth.
  • The spread/shape guess was wrong. The simulator has the same spread for every row, and the true model with that assumption wins. Residual size does not track the fitted mean (rank correlation ≈ 0).
  • The mean model is the problem. About 65–75% of v5's error is the part-(1) model, and almost all of that is the mean fit. v5 moved weight from "who is in the data" to "how they respond" in nearly equal and opposite amounts. A likely reason: period B has heavy-tailed or skewed inputs, and a binned booster trained on period A's inputs goes flat beyond its bins, so the shift in inputs is missed. A straight-line fit removes the bias.
Caveat. This check used the same seeds that failed, and we picked the linear fit after seeing the failure. That is why it is only a reason to run a fresh test, not a result. The simulator's true mean is a straight line, so a pass would not show that the approach copes with curved means.

What is next: one frozen test

Hypothesis. Swap only v5's mean model for an ordinary least-squares line (per period, intercept plus all inputs, nothing tuned). Then skew_comp and tail_comp, at n=2000 and n=20000, are each BEST or NON-INFERIOR against the best of CFM/DFL, with weight in the right role.

Fresh seeds. 9700–9749 (n=2000, 50 reps) and 9800–9819 (n=20000, 20 reps). Never used before. The failed seeds (9500–9549) and the closed gate's sealed seeds (9600–9619) are not reused.

Bars (unchanged from PREREG_V4 §5): BEST if the one-sided 95% upper bound of the paired difference is below 0; NON-INFERIOR if below δ = 10% of the planted effect (0.004025 skew, 0.000459 tail); t-interval and 10 000-draw bootstrap must agree; Holm across the four cells.

Kill rule. Any cell fails, misassigns the role, or is incomplete → park v5. No retuning, no new seeds.
