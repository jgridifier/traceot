TRACE v5.1 coverage: the 90% band covers about 90% of the time

PASS   2026-11-13, 3:16–3:22 PM ET · prereg dfaaf72 · runner merge 14754e6

Short version. After a 2026-11-11 hold and the owner's dated approval on 2026-11-13, we ran the frozen coverage test once, on its own seeds. A 90% bootstrap band was placed around the composition piece and around the response piece, on 400 fresh draws of each of two straight-line cells. All four units landed inside the pre-registered pass interval (344–374 covers out of 400). The band claim passes. This says nothing new about how accurate the v5.1 point estimates are — that claim was already settled by the v5.1 gate.

What was tested

When an outcome's distribution changes between two periods, TRACE splits the change into composition (the inputs moved) and response (the way the outcome reacts to the inputs changed). The v5.1 gate showed that, on two straight-line cells where all of the planted change is composition, the point estimates land in the right bucket and beat the rivals. This follow-up asks a different question: if we draw a 90% band around each piece, does that band contain the frozen truth about 90% of the time?

The band is the one frozen in the pre-registration: a two-sample percentile bootstrap, 500 resamples, 5th to 95th percentile, no bias correction. A replication is covered when the truth sits inside the interval. The pass rule is a binomial interval around 90% on 400 draws: 344 to 374 covers. Too few or too many both fail. All four units (two cells × two pieces) must pass. There is no Holm adjustment.

Pooled coverage, reported only, with no bar: 1,442 / 1,600 (0.90125). Failed bootstrap refits: 0. Wall time: 350 seconds on 8 workers, well under the 60-minute cap. Every one of the 800 tasks finished. Every seed was in 10400–10799. The sealed range 9600–9619 was not touched.

What this does not say

  • It does not change the v5.1 accuracy claim. That still rests on the four-cell gate.
  • It is only about these two straight-line cells at 2,000 rows and 5 inputs. It says nothing about 20,000 rows, structure cells, mixed cells, or curved means.
  • No library function for the band is shipped here. A later change that adds one has to reproduce this exact band.

The owner approved the study on 2026-11-13 at 2:59 PM ET, in his own words: “You have my approval for all trace stuff - you are running the lab on this - I’m more an advisor on that project.” The runner was merged first and ran nothing (PR #57).
