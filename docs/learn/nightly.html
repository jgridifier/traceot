1. Tonight’s takeaway

The curved-mean test failed, and the claim is parked. Swapping V5.1’s straight-line mean for a fixed spline did not hold up: it lost or tied too weakly on every heavy-tail cell, two cells could not even be built, and on all four cells where only the response changed it still invented a composition piece bigger than the margin. TRACE’s only validated claim remains V5.1 on straight-line means, and a composition piece on a curved problem should be read as possibly a fitting artifact.

3. The practical job (refresher)

Two periods, A and B. Each is a table of units (rows), features (columns), and one outcome whose distribution moved. TRACE’s job is a readable budget of why:

  • Mix (composition): the kinds of units showing up changed.
  • Response (structure): the same kinds of units now produce different outcomes.
  • Entry / exit: units that arrived or left, kept on their own line when IDs are known.
  • Noise: bands and a no-shift test say whether any of the above is bigger than sampling wobble. The v5.x line does not print bands yet, so read its splits as point estimates.

Today’s failure is about the second bullet bleeding into the first. When only the response changed, the right composition piece is zero. The spline version still reported one, above the margin, at both sample sizes. That is the error that matters most in practice, because it tells you the population changed when it didn’t.

4. Worked vignette: two years of a city 10K

Illustrative only: no TRACE run behind this story, and no numbers.

A city 10K publishes finish times and runner ages for two years. The second year’s slow tail is fatter. Either more beginners signed up (a mix change), or race day was hot and everyone slowed at the same fitness (a response change). People who ran only one of the two years go on the entry / exit lines.

Suppose the truth is a pure hot day: the field was the same kind of people, just slower. A good explainer reports nearly all response and close to zero mix. What today’s gate says is that, when finish time curves against age and the change lives in the tails, the spline version can still hand a visible slice to mix. You would walk away thinking the race attracted more beginners, which is the wrong lesson for the organizers.

Practical rule: before believing a mix piece, ask whether the inputs actually moved. Compare the age distribution between years directly. If it barely changed but TRACE reports a sizeable mix piece on a curved, heavy-tailed outcome, treat that piece as suspect.

5. How to read a TRACE output tomorrow

  • Budget exactness is bookkeeping health (pieces sum to the total). It says nothing about accuracy.
  • Check which mean model ran. explain_v5_1 (least squares) is the only v5.x version with a passed gate, and only on straight-line means. mean_model="spline" now exists but carries no validated claim. The default booster failed its gate.
  • A composition piece is not always real. This is no longer a worry; it is measured. On structure-only problems with curved means, the spline version reported false composition above the margin in all four cells.
  • Heavy tails are the weak spot. Every curved tail_comp cell failed. If the shift is mostly in the extremes, lean on the rivals (DFL, CFM) as a cross-check.
  • Overlap. Inputs one period never saw make the split lean on extrapolation. TRACE still does not print an overlap share.

7. One paper / idea for tonight

Chernozhukov, V., Fernández-Val, I., & Melly, B. (2013). Inference on counterfactual distributions. Econometrica 81(6), 2205–2268. doi:10.3982/ECTA10582.

This is the paper behind CFM, the rival TRACE keeps being measured against. Its main contribution is not the point split but honest uncertainty: it shows the whole counterfactual distribution can be estimated with uniform confidence bands that hold across every quantile at once, built by a bootstrap that is valid for the full curve rather than one point at a time.

Why tonight: after today, the cheapest way to make TRACE more useful is not a fancier mean, it is telling you when a piece is noise. A false composition piece like today’s would be harmless if its band clearly included zero. The open question for us is whether bands on V5.1 can hit the coverage bar that v3’s bands missed (0.815 pooled).
