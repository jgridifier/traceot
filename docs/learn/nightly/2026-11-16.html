MERGED · DECISION

Next suite chosen: struct cells (PR #70)

PREREG_V5_1_STRUCT.md picks struct cells from the four out-of-scope suites. The reason: right now a method that labelled every shift “composition” would also pass the V5.1 gate. Cells where the composition truth is about 0 (structure-only), or where both roles are planted with opposite signs (mixed), are the direct test of that. A PASS widens the claim from “BEST on composition cells” to “separates composition from structure across the H7 shape family”.

Why the other suites are not first: ShiftBench needs a new channel adapter and has a heteroskedastic scenario that V5.1 excludes by design. Speed alone isn’t a claim. Per-feature shares need a new oracle and a new rival, and the doc names them as the next suite after this one.
