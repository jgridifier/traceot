Pre-registration

V5.2 specified-family non-linear mean (PR #54, merged 2026-11-13 06:31 EST, CI green on rust and python 3.10/3.13, main cc8c1d7). The header status reads PRE-REGISTERED · NOT APPROVED · NOT RUN. Running it needs your own dated approval quoted in the header of PREREG_V5_2_SPECIFIED.md. No runner has been written.

  • Where it comes from: the known limitation in PREREG_V5_1 §7. The v5.1 simulator's mean is linear, so a linear location fit is correct by construction, and robustness to curved means was never shown.
  • The single change: each period's location fit becomes ordinary least squares on the v5.1 linear terms plus the two exact curved terms of the true mean (x₃² and sin(x₅)). There's no penalty, no knots, and nothing to tune. Everything downstream (residual law, four hybrids, L-moments, two-player Shapley split) is unchanged.
  • The simulator: the curvature sits only on columns the composition scenarios do not shift. That keeps "curved mean" separate from "extrapolating a curve along a shifted covariate".
  • The gate: the same four cells as V5.1 (skew_comp, tail_comp × n = 2000 / 20 000) and the same BEST / NON-INFERIOR bars plus the role check. It uses fresh seeds 11000–11049 and 11100–11119.

This is not a second spline gate. The earlier V5.2 spline test failed, and its park stays in force. The new hypothesis is taken from V5.1 §7, not from that run's cell outcomes. Seeds 9600–9619 stay sealed, and 10400–10799 stay reserved for the held coverage study. The plain-language page is at v5_2_specified_prereg.html.

Reading a shift report

Why this matters when you read a report. TRACE splits a change into composition (who is in the data) and response (how outcomes behave given inputs). It does that by fitting a location model per period and treating what's left as the residual law. If the true mean is curved but the fit is a straight line, the missed curve lands in the residuals. A shape change can then be attributed to the wrong piece. For now, v5.1 attributions are only claimed where a linear mean is plausible. The report-only linear_mean_check diagnostic (PR #47) is a hint, not a pass/fail. The new pre-registration is the first planned test of what happens when the location model is curved and correct.
