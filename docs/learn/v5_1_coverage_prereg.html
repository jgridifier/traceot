Where we are. v5.1 shipped on a passed accuracy gate: its composition / response split was the most accurate of the methods compared on the four straight-line-mean test cells. It has no tested uncertainty band. The v5.1 pre-registration listed “coverage” as out of scope. This page is that item, and only that item.

The question

If we resample the data and report the middle 90% of the re-computed composition and response pieces, does that range contain the true value in about 90 out of 100 fresh datasets? If it does much less often, the band is too narrow and would mislead. If it does much more often, it is too wide.

The one hypothesis

On the two straight-line-mean composition cells (skew_comp, tail_comp; 2,000 rows per period, 5 inputs), the 90% percentile bootstrap band around v5.1’s composition piece and around its response piece covers the true value at a rate inside a binomial pass interval fixed in advance, in each of the four (cell × piece) combinations.

Why expect it? Every step of v5.1 is smooth: a least-squares line per period, the leftover noise, a mixture of the two, and exact L-moments. For smooth estimators like this the ordinary bootstrap is expected to work. The test checks that expectation. It was chosen from the v5.1 out-of-scope list and that reasoning, not from the v5.2 run (v5.2 used curved means and computed no bands).

The band (fixed now)

  • Point estimate: traceot.explain_v5_1 with the shipped defaults; nothing tuned.
  • 500 bootstrap redraws per dataset: resample rows of period A and of period B separately, with replacement, and refit.
  • Band = the 5th to 95th percentile of the 500 refits. No bias correction.
  • Graded channel as in the gate: third L-moment (skew) for skew_comp, fourth (tail weight) for tail_comp.
  • Truth: the frozen population values already in the repo (2 million draws). If more than 5 of the 500 refits crash, that dataset counts as a miss.

The new range overlaps none of them. 9600–9619 stay sealed and are not touched.

Kill rule. If any unit falls outside its interval or the run times out, the band claim is parked (PARK_V5_1_COVERAGE.md), v5.1 keeps saying “no validated band”, and nothing is re-run, re-seeded or re-tuned. The v5.1 accuracy result does not change either way.

Honest limits

  • Two cells, one size (2,000 rows), straight-line means only. Even a PASS says nothing about 20,000 rows, the structure cells, the mixed cell, or curved means.
  • The percentile band does not correct bias. If the point estimate is biased, coverage will show it; that is part of the test.
  • Simulated data only.
