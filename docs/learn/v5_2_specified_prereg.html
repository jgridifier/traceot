TRACE: does the mixture still work when the mean is curved and the location family is right?

Where we are. V5.1 passed its four-cell gate with a straight-line mean model, on a simulator whose mean is itself a straight line. That result, in the V5.1 pre-registration’s own words, “does not show robustness to non-linear means.” A later attempt (v5.2) tried a five-knot spline approximation under curved means, failed, and is parked. This page is not a second spline test. It asks the narrower question V5.1 already posed: if the mean is curved and the location model is the correct family, does the same mixture still pass the same gate?

The one hypothesis

When the true mean is a smooth non-linear additive function of the inputs, and the location model is ordinary least squares on the straight-line terms plus the exact two curved terms that define that mean, the v5.1 mixture with only that location fit changed is BEST or NON-INFERIOR against DFL and CFM, and passes the role check, on both pure-composition cells (skew_comp, tail_comp) at both sizes (2,000 rows and 20,000 rows).

The method change

V5.1 fits a straight line per period, \(\hat\mu_t(x)=\hat\beta_{0,t}+x^\top\hat\beta_t\). This test replaces that with

\[ \hat\mu_t(x)=\hat\beta_{0,t}+x^\top\hat\beta_t+\hat\gamma_{1,t}\,x_3^2+\hat\gamma_{2,t}\sin(x_5), \]

the least-squares fit on the same intercept and columns, plus those two known curved terms. The true mean uses exactly those terms (a scaled \(X_3^2-1\) and a scaled \(\sin X_5\)), so the family is correctly specified. Everything after the fit is unchanged: the leftover-noise law, the four hybrid mixtures, the 256-point grid, the L-moments, and the two-player Shapley split. The work lives in the Rust core, with a thin Python switch mean_model="specified". No neural nets, no smoothing parameter, no tuning.

The curve is placed only on columns that the two composition cells do not shift, so the test is about a non-linear mean, not about extrapolating a quadratic into a heavy tail. Under the period-A law the curved part has variance about 1.86, next to 2.25 from the straight-line part, so it is not a small perturbation.

How it is graded

ItemFixed value
Cells4: skew_comp and tail_comp, at n = 2,000 (p = 5) and n = 20,000 (p = 20)
Datasets50 at the smaller size (indices 11000–11049); 20 at the larger (11100–11119)
ComparatorBest of DFL and CFM, paired on the same draws
AccuracyOne-sided 95% upper bound of mean (error − rival error), Student-t and a 10,000-draw bootstrap; the larger bound is used. BEST if that bound is < 0; NON-INFERIOR if it is < δ = 10% of the planted size of the graded channel
Role checkThe composition piece is larger, in absolute mean, than the response piece, and swapping the two roles does not lower the error
Gate PASSAll four cells BEST or NON-INFERIOR after Holm, and all four role checks pass
Time cap30 minutes on 8 cores; unfinished work is never filled in

The new range overlaps none of them. 9600–9619 stay sealed and are not touched. 10400–10799 stay reserved for the held coverage study and are not touched.

Kill rule. If any cell misses the accuracy bar, fails the role check, or runs out of time, the claim is parked (PARK_V5_2_SPECIFIED.md), and nothing is re-run, re-seeded, or re-tuned. V5.1’s straight-line claim is unchanged either way, and the v5.2 park stays in place. If the simulator cannot even be calibrated, the gate seeds are not run and the hypothesis is left undecided — it is not redesigned.

What this will not claim

  • A PASS does not say a spline, a GAM, or any approximating model works. The location family is correct by construction; that is the point of the test, and its limit.
  • It does not un-park v5.2, and it does not re-run v5.2’s seeds.
  • It does not run the held coverage study, does not register the proposed √n alternative, and does not give linear_mean_check a threshold.
  • Structure-only cells, mixed cells, speed, and real data are out of scope.
