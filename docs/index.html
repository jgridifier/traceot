  • ADOPT, explained: what optimal-transport regression is, how additive transport works, and our reproduction of the ADOPT paper's simulations.
  • TRACE, beside explainable boosting: what EBMs are, how TRACE models a whole conditional distribution, and the leakage and oddness checks.
  • TRACE theory: L-moments, per-feature attribution, monotonicity, why a tail feature can still be missed, shift explanations, distribution-valued rows, and Wasserstein-robust fits.
  • Explaining a shift: how a change in a distribution splits into composition and structure, per channel and per feature, and what the gap between the two averages means.

Roadmap

  1. Milestone 1 (this release): faithful ADOPT (Song & Müller, AISTATS 2026) with a numpy reference and a matching Rust core.
  2. TRACE v0 (unpublished): an EBM-style additive model of the conditional quantile function, with location, scale and shape channels, oddness scores, and leakage and placebo checks. ADOPT stays in the tree as a reproduction only. Designed for millions of rows. See the learning page.
