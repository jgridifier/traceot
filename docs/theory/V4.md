## 0. Object

The v4 object is the **model mixture law** of a hybrid \((s,t)\):

\[
F_{[s,t]}(y) \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{x\sim F_s}\, F_t(y\mid x),\qquad
Q_{[s,t]} \;=\; F_{[s,t]}^{-1},
\]

with \(F_t(\cdot\mid x)\) the CDF inverse of TRACE's monotone conditional quantile
\(Q_t(\cdot\mid x)\). Reports use the shifted-Legendre / L-moment channels
\(P^*_k\) on \([0,1]\), weights \(w_k = 1/(2k+1)\). Write the exact split

\[
Q_{[s,t]}(u) \;=\; \bar Q[s,t](u) + M[s,t](u),
\]

where \(\bar Q[s,t](u) = \mathbb{E}_{x\sim F_s} Q_t(u\mid x)\) is the **barycenter**
(Vincent average of quantile functions) and \(M = Q_{[s,t]} - \bar Q[s,t]\) is the
**mixing term** (Lagrangian–Eulerian gap).

## 1. Mixture target and channel Parseval

**Lemma 1 (Parseval; SHIFT_V2 Prop. 1) [P].** For \(D = \sum_{k<K} d_k P^*_k\),
\(\|D\|^2_{L^2[0,1]} = \sum_k w_k d_k^2\).

Applied to the model-marginal quantile gap \(D_M = Q_{[B,B]} - Q_{[A,A]}\),

\[
W_2^2(F_{[A,A]}, F_{[B,B]}) \;=\; \sum_{k<K} w_k\, (\Delta c^M_k)^2
\;+\; \|R\|^2,
\]

where \(\Delta c^M_k\) are the Legendre coefficients of \(D_M\) and \(R\) is the
truncation residual (Theorem 2 of UNIQUENESS handles \(R\) as an extra channel).

## 2. Mixing-player game (M1)

### 2.1 Second-order expansion of \(M\) [P, refined by D1]

Assume a location–scale conditional law within a period,
\(Y = \mu(X) + \sigma\varepsilon\), \(\varepsilon\) with density \(f\) and quantile \(q\),
\(\delta = \mu(X) - \mathbb{E}\mu\), \(v = \mathrm{Var}\,\delta\), and \(f'/f\), \(f''/f\)
bounded on the quantile range used. Then

\[
Q_M(u) \;=\; \bar Q(u) \;-\; \frac{v}{2\sigma}\,\frac{f'(q(u))}{f(q(u))}
\;+\; O\!\bigl(\mathbb{E}|\delta|^3/\sigma^2\bigr).
\]

*Proof sketch.* Expand \(F_M(y) = \mathbb{E} F((y-\bar\mu-\delta)/\sigma)\) to second order
in \(\delta\) using \(\mathbb{E}\delta=0\); invert at level \(u\). For Gaussian \(\varepsilon\),
\(-f'/f = q\), so \(Q_M = \bar Q + (v/(2\sigma)) q\), the first-order expansion of the
exact \(\sqrt{\sigma^2+v}\). ∎

**Claim rewrite (D1) [T].** The remainder after the displayed correction scales as
\(O((s/\sigma)^3)\) when \(\mathbb{E}|\delta|^3\) is of order \(s^3\), and as
\(O((s/\sigma)^4)\) when \(\delta\) is symmetric (all odd moments vanish — the cubic
term is identically zero). On \(\varepsilon\in\{N(0,1),\mathrm{skew}\text{-}N(a=4),\mathrm{Gumbel}\}\)
with \(\delta\sim N(0,s^2)\) and \(s/\sigma\in\{0.05,0.1,0.2,0.4\}\), log–log slopes of the
sup-residual lie in \([2.4, 4.5]\); at \(s/\sigma=0.1\) the correction removes
\(\ge 90\%\) of the raw barycenter–mixture gap (and \(\ge 85\%\) in the Legendre-channel
version). See `V4_DERIVATIONS.txt` D1.*.

### 2.2 Per-feature split of mixing via covariance [P]

For additive \(\mu = b + \sum_j f_{0j}\),

\[
v \;=\; \sum_j \mathrm{Cov}(f_{0j}(X_j), \mu(X)).
\]

Each covariance term is the feature's share of the variance (identity; equals the
Shapley value of the variance game restricted to its covariance form; reduces to
\(\mathrm{Var}\, f_{0j}\) under independence). Mixing piece per feature and channel:

\[
m_{kj} \;\propto\; w_k \cdot \mathrm{Cov}_s(f_{0j},\mu_t)\cdot\langle s_t, P^*_k\rangle,
\quad s_t = f'_t(q_t)/f_t(q_t).
\]

Beyond second order the residual is **reported, not hidden**.

### 2.3 Reference-symmetric switch game on marginal quantiles [P]

Players: composition / structure per (channel, feature), plus composition-mix /
structure-mix. The reference-symmetric switch game of UNIQUENESS Theorem 4 applied
to the *marginal* quantile functions has

\[
V_{\mathrm{sym}}(S) \;=\; \langle D_M,\, Q_S - Q_A\rangle,
\]

which is linear in \(Q_S\). Therefore Theorem 4's proof extends verbatim: the value
is efficient for the model-marginal \(W_2^2\), period-swap antisymmetric, and
order-invariant. Barycenter players keep their \(\Phi\) values projected onto \(D_M\);
mixing players receive \(\langle D_M, \Delta M\rangle\) split by the two-player Shapley rule.

## 3. Bin-histogram balancing (M4)

**Proposition (bin-marginal sufficiency; D2) [P, T].** For the additive barycenter,
\(G_{kj}[s,t] = \mathbb{E}_{x\sim F_s} f_{t,kj}(x_j)\) with \(f\) piecewise constant on TRACE's
bins. If weights \(w\) on period-\(t\) rows satisfy

\[
\sum_i w_i\, 1\{x_{ij}\in b\} \;=\; P_s(x_j\in b)
\]

for every feature \(j\) and bin \(b\), then \(\sum_i w_i f_{t,kj}(x_{ij}) = G_{kj}[s,t]\)
exactly for every \(k,j\) and every such \(f\).

*Proof.* Linearity: \(f\) is a linear combination of bin indicators. ∎

Verified: IPF/raking weights achieve \(\max|\sum w f - \mathbb{E}_s f| \le 10^{-15}\) over
100 random piecewise-constant \(f\) (`D2.raking_exact_for_bin_piecewise_f`). Logistic
odds weights on a linear index fail the same check (`D2.logistic_odds_negative_control_fails`).

**Entropy balancing** [R35] gives the minimum-KL weights meeting the bin-marginal
constraints (Gibbs form). **Mixture target** needs joint balance: Riesz regression
[R28] with dictionary = bin indicators + pairwise products of the top-\(m\) features
(\(m=10\)), closed-form ridge least squares minimizing \(\mathbb{E}_t[\alpha^2] - 2\mathbb{E}_s[\alpha]\).

## 4. Fit what we report (M2)

1. **Channel 0 by squared loss.** \(\int Q\, P^*_0\,du = \mathbb{E}[Y\mid x]\), so
   least-squares boosting of \(y\) gives the \(L^2\) channel 0 directly [P].
2. **Channels \(k\ge 1\) by PIT pseudo-outcomes.** Exact identity
   \(c_k(x) = \mathbb{E}[(2k+1)\, Y\, P^*_k(U)\mid x]\), \(U=F(Y\mid x)\) for continuous laws
   (FIXES2). Estimate \(\hat U\) from cross-fitted \(\hat F(Y\mid x)\) (2 folds, 2 iterations).
3. **Sobolev ridge across \(k\):** penalty \(\lambda\sum_k k^{2s}\|f_{kj}\|^2\) (JKO-type
   proximal step in flat \(W_2\) geometry [R1]); \(\lambda\) by CV on CRPS.
4. **Recalibrate** by PIT-isotonic distributional regression [R41] or distributional
   conformal prediction [R50].

## 5. Refit-free orthogonal inference (M3)

**Proposition (split-product unbiasedness; D3) [P, T].** Split each period into
independent halves; let \(\hat D^{(1)}, \hat D^{(2)}\) estimate \(D\). Then

\[
\mathbb{E}\langle\hat D^{(1)}, \hat D^{(2)}\rangle \;=\; \|D\|^2,
\]

while \(\mathbb{E}\|\hat D\|^2 = \|D\|^2 + \mathrm{tr}\,\mathrm{Var}(\hat D)\).

Verified over 20 000 MC reps: mean cross-product within 3 MC-SE of \(\|D\|^2\);
plug-in matches \(\|D\|^2+\mathrm{tr}\,\mathrm{Var}\) (`D3.*`).

**Symmetrised players.** \(\Phi_{kjr} = \tfrac12 w_k\bigl(\Delta\bar c_k^{(1)} a_{kjr}^{(2)} + \Delta\bar c_k^{(2)} a_{kjr}^{(1)}\bigr)\)
sum exactly to the cross-product budget (algebraic identity, err \(\le 10^{-12}\)).

**Bands.** Bin-level influence functions → multiplier bootstrap ([R57], \(B=2000\),
Rademacher), studentized. **Null:** studentized label-shuffle test [R38]. Fallback:
cheap bootstrap with \(t\)-quantiles [R56] for non-smooth pieces (PAV, trim).

## 6. Entry / exit with growth–death (M7)

When unit identities are observed: exact mass weights (balanced decomposition).
When not: unbalanced OT on the 1-D outcome (Hellinger–Kantorovich / Chizat et al.
[R17], arXiv 1508.05216) with stated \(\rho\); growth/death mass is its own player.
Lesson from Waddington-OT / moscot [R14–R16]: unbalanced penalties are a tuning
choice only when identities are latent.

## 7. Time-continuous decomposition (M8) — only what D4 supports

**Claim rewrite (D4) [T].** Along the diagonal path \(\sigma(\tau)=\tau\) of the
multilinear extension of the \(G\)-tables (equivalently: Aumann–Shapley / integrated
gradients on the \(2pK\) player coordinates),

\[
\mathrm{AS}_{kjr} \;=\; \Phi_{kjr}
\quad\text{if and only if}\quad
I_{kj} \;=\; 0 \;\text{for all }k,j,
\]

where \(I_{kj} = G[B,B]-G[B,A]-G[A,B]+G[A,A]\) is the composition×structure
interaction. When every \(I_{kj}=0\), equality holds to \(\le 10^{-14}\). When some
\(I_{kj}\ne 0\), a nonzero gap appears (observed \(\max|\Phi-\mathrm{AS}|\approx 0.44\)
on a random table with \(\max|I|\approx 2.27\); both rules remain efficient for
\(W=\sum_k w_k\Delta_k^2\)).

**Do not claim** general path-attribution equivalence. The continuous-time reading
of TRACE's budget is: *AS along the geodesic equals \(\Phi\) iff all interactions
vanish*. (Anchored switch-game Shapley / Owen can differ from \(\Phi\) for the same
reason — UNIQUENESS Theorem 3.)

## 10. Scope and non-claims

- Per-feature mixing split is exact in *total*; the covariance attribution is
  second-order accurate in the split (residual reported).
- Interactions outside the additive model remain in the (f)-style residual.
- Theorems are algebraic on the fitted model; statistical rates are separate
  (prereg bars in `PREREG_V4.md`).
- No industry- or application-specific language; neutral simulated data only.
